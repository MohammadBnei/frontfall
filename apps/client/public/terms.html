Frontfall is free to play and is in beta: rules, maps and features change, and games or accounts may be reset. It is offered as it is, with no promise that the server is always up, that a game will finish or that your name will be kept. Nothing is sold, and nothing will give an advantage in a game.

The source code is under the MIT license: you may read it, copy it and run your own server. The license also says the software comes without warranty.

How to behave

  • Choose a name that is not offensive and does not pass you off as someone else.
  • Do not cheat: no modified client that gives an edge, no scripts that play for you, no using bugs to harm other players' games.
  • Do not harass or insult other players, in names or in quick messages, and do not disrupt the server (flooding, trying to break in).
  • Play one account per person; do not sell or share an account.

Players can report others from the player menu of an online game, and the operator may refuse a name, end a game, delete an account or ban a network address, for a few days or for good, when these rules are broken. Bans are described in the privacy policy.
